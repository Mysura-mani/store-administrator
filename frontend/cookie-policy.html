Cookie Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This reflects what the Service sets today. If we later add analytics, marketing, or any other tracking, this page (and a real cookie-consent banner) will be updated before that happens.

Tips Tracker, operated by HX46, uses exactly one cookie, described below. We don't use analytics, advertising, or tracking cookies of any kind.

The cookie we set

NamePurposeTypeExpires
tips.sid Keeps you signed in between page loads (which branch, and whether you're staff, admin, or owner). Strictly necessary — the Service cannot function without it. 12 hours, or when you log out.

This cookie is set directly by this Service (not by any third party), is marked HttpOnly (inaccessible to page scripts) and SameSite=Lax, and carries no personal information itself — just a random session identifier that maps to your login state on our server.

Cookies from Google Sign-In

If the owner uses the optional "Sign in with Google" button, that redirect briefly takes you to a accounts.google.com page, which sets its own cookies under Google's domain according to Google's own Cookie Policy. We don't control those and they're not readable by this Service.

Local storage

Your browser's local storage (not a cookie, but similar in spirit) is used to remember that you've dismissed the cookie notice and your light/dark theme choice, so neither needs to be authenticated or sent to the server. Nothing else is stored there.

Managing cookies

Because tips.sid is required to log in at all, blocking it will prevent the Service from working. You can still clear it any time via your browser's settings, or by using the "Log out" button in the app.
