Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This is a general policy covering how the Tips Tracker application works today. It isn't a substitute for advice from a qualified professional about your specific obligations under Irish and EU data protection law.

This Privacy Policy explains what information HX46 ("we", "us") collects through the Tips Tracker application (the "Service"), how it's used, and who can see it.

Who this applies to

The Service is an internal tool used by our own branch staff, branch admins, and the business owner to record tips and delivery pay. It is not aimed at the general public and does not knowingly collect information from customers or visitors browsing our business's public-facing website (if separate from this tool).

What we collect

  • Account credentials. Branch staff/admin passwords and the owner password, stored as one-way cryptographic hashes (bcrypt) — never in plain text, and never visible to us or anyone else after they're set.
  • Operational records you enter. Employee and driver names, hours worked, tip amounts, delivery counts, and optional notes, entered by staff/admins as part of normal use.
  • Owner sign-in via Google (optional). If the owner chooses to enable "Sign in with Google", we receive that Google account's verified email address from Google, used only to confirm it matches the one Gmail address the owner has authorized. We do not receive or store the owner's Google password.
  • Session data. A single cookie that keeps you signed in (see our Cookie Policy).

How we use it

Solely to operate the Service: authenticating logins, calculating and displaying tip/delivery pay, and — if the owner has connected it — mirroring the operational records above into a Google Sheet under the owner's own Google account for reporting. We do not sell this data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties beyond the services named below.

Third-party services we use

  • Google (Sign-In and Sheets API) — only if the owner opts in, as described above. Google's own privacy practices apply to how Google itself handles that data; see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Chart.js (via jsdelivr CDN) — a script file loaded from jsdelivr's CDN to draw charts on the owner's Analytics page. This loads a script file only; it does not transmit any of your data to jsdelivr.
  • Vercel (hosting) — the Service itself runs on Vercel's infrastructure. Vercel processes the same operational data described above as our hosting provider; see Vercel's Privacy Policy.

We do not use analytics trackers, advertising pixels, or any other third-party embed beyond what's listed above.

Where data is stored

Operational records and password hashes are stored on the server that runs this Service, hosted on Vercel. If Google Sheet sync is enabled, a copy of the operational records (never passwords) also lives in a Google Sheet owned by the business owner's own Google account.

Data retention

We keep records for as long as the Service is in use, so pay history remains accurate and auditable. Deleting a branch through the owner dashboard permanently deletes that branch's employees, drivers, and history. Where an employee or driver stops working for us, their historical pay records are kept only as long as needed for our own payroll and tax record-keeping obligations, after which they're deleted along with the branch data that contains them.

Your rights

If you're in Ireland or elsewhere in the EU, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) gives you the right to access, correct, or request deletion of information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain uses of it. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at pmysura1166@gmail.com. If you believe we haven't handled your data properly, you can also complain to Ireland's Data Protection Commission at www.dataprotection.ie.
